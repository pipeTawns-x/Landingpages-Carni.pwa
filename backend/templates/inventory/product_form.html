{% extends "panel/base.html" %} {% comment %} Products, create and edit (/panel/productos/nuevo/ and /panel/productos//editar/). It is kit/productos/form.html as the redesign delivered it (pruebas 2d7cf5d3, sync point S3), applied over the light template of the M13. Fieldsets stand in for tabs, and there is no JavaScript. Context of the view: product_form, spec_form, is_update and, when editing, product. The controls are drawn by Django with the classes that inventory/forms.py gives each widget, inside the wrappers of the design (the "$" and the unit are text over the padding of the control, and the arrow of a select is an icon). A form without action posts to the page it is on. The price per pound is not a field: it is shown as text and recalculated on every save. "Confirmar cambio de precio o cantidad mínima" is not a field of the form either: it is the checkbox the view reads as confirm=1, and it is a shortcut, because without it a change of the price or of the minimum quantity stops at the confirmation page.
